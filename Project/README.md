# Student Particle Physics Research Grants

**Department of Physics, University of Tirana — Introduction to Particle Physics**

This is a teaching funding programme; it does not reproduce the rules of an external funding agency.

Teams of 4–5 students propose, build, commission, and use an instrument to answer a particle physics question. The maximum request is **EUR 15,000 per team** (draft interpretation: confirm before publication). A justified smaller request is welcome; spending the ceiling earns no advantage.

## Workflow

**Concept discussion → proposal and oral defence → conditional award/revision → construction → commissioning review → measurements and analysis → scientific and financial reports → demonstration and presentation.**

Copy the templates into your team's project repository. Keep raw data unchanged, record settings and live time, and document how to regenerate figures. Use tagged releases or commit identifiers for submissions; the instructor will specify the submission route and repository visibility. Do not publish receipts, bank details, personal contact information, or participant assessments in a public repository. Supply receipts privately to the instructor and link only receipt IDs in the public financial report.

The example is a guide to reasoning and level of detail, not a project students must copy. Its costs, geometry, performance parameters, schedule, and financial transactions are fictional teaching assumptions. Its plots are model predictions, not measurements. Existing equipment must be booked and checked; listing an instrument here does not establish its specifications or availability.
