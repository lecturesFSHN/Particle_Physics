# Assessment rubric

The first four marks are shared team marks. The individual mark may differ between students. Total = 0.15 proposal + 0.25 apparatus + 0.30 measurement + 0.10 documentation + 0.20 individual, with each component scored out of 100.

| Component | Strong evidence | Evidence needing improvement |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal, 15% | Clear question; justified design, rate/exposure estimate, budget and achievable milestones | Vague objective; unsupported performance or prices; missing backgrounds |
| Apparatus and commissioning, 25% | Substantial student construction/integration; documented signals, controls and performance; usable handover | Assembly without validation; unexplained instability; undocumented settings |
| Measurement and interpretation, 30% | Traceable data; appropriate statistical and systematic treatment; defensible comparison and limitations | Counting without live time/background checks; unsupported particle identification; overstated conclusions |
| Documentation and dissemination, 10% | Reproducible files, readable report, financial reconciliation, useful presentation and operating guide | Missing raw data/code; unclear plots; inconsistent accounts; equipment cannot be reused |
| Individual, 20% | Demonstrated contribution and understanding of the full chain from signal to physics conclusion | Understanding confined to an isolated task; contribution cannot be established |

Use the oral defence, logbook, task records and contribution statements together. Include a question outside each student's primary role. Do not equate commit volume with effort. Do not penalize an unexpected result merely for disagreeing with the prediction; assess the quality of investigation and evidence.
